WordPress & WooCommerce

SEV-0 / SEV-1

Readiness: Immediate SWAT Triage — First response SEV-0 ≤ 15 min · SEV-1 ≤ 30 min

Outage Vectors:

  • White screen after a plugin or theme update (PHP fatal error)
  • Payment captured but the order stuck in 'Pending payment'

TMA SWAT: Locating the fault through debug.log and PHP-FPM logs, isolating plugin conflicts by bisection on a staging copy, reconciling orders against payment records, and moving WP-Cron to a real server cron.

Scope of intervention: We work on all the code on your server: theme, plugins, WooCommerce hooks and the database. Commercial plugins belong to their vendors; if the fault is inside one, it is reported to the vendor with evidence and a workaround is applied in your own code.
