Idempotency (Çift İşlem Koruması)

A mathematical and API principle ensuring identical requests executed multiple times yield the exact same result without duplicate side-effects. What it means for agencies: Prevents charging a customer credit card multiple times when they impatiently click checkout repeatedly.

Aciliyet: Yüksek

Tanım
