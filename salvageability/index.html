5 Critical Dimensions Evaluated

1. Architectural Coupling & Spaghetti Density

Does changing one module break unrelated endpoints? Monolithic tangles and hidden dependencies.

2. Test Coverage & Domain Knowledge

Did the original developers vanish? Are automated test suites nonexistent, leaving code untested until production?

3. Database Integrity & Schema Hygiene

Are foreign keys and constraints intact, or is the schema fraught with deadlocks and orphaned data?

4. Live Traffic Pressure & Deadline Urgency

Is the system currently processing real revenue with tight contractual delivery milestones?

5. Technical Debt Drag Ratio

Does shipping a minor feature take weeks instead of hours due to crippling maintenance overhead?
