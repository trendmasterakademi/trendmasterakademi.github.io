Published Incident Post-Mortems

  • → PM-01 · Friday 18:40 — Payment Webhook Storm, Database Deadlock, and Connection Pool Starvation SEV-1 CRITICAL · Database & Concurrency

    During a high-traffic campaign, thousands of concurrent payment webhooks hit the backend simultaneously, opening unbuffered database transactions that deadlocked on unindexed foreign keys and exhausted PostgreSQL connection pools.

    Duration: 42 minutes · Impact: 1,420 failed checkout transactions, 42 minutes of HTTP 500/504 errors, severe support escalation.
  • → PM-02 · Flash Sale Double Charges and Concurrent Balance Race Conditions SEV-1 CRITICAL · Payment & Distributed Systems

    During a flash sale, impatient users multi-clicked the payment button. Due to lack of backend request deduplication and distributed locking, concurrent payment gateway charges were executed for identical cart states.

    Duration: 14 minutes · Impact: $18,400 in 74 duplicate credit card charges within 14 minutes, chargeback warning from payment gateway.
  • → PM-03 · Node.js API OOM Crash Every 6 Hours: The Undetected Event Listener Leak SEV-2 MAJOR · Memory & Performance

    A SaaS production backend suffered recurring Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes every 5.5 to 6 hours as RSS heap climbed past 1.4GB. The internal team had resorted to a cron job rebooting the server every 4 hours.

    Duration: 3-week chronic bug → Solved in 4 hours · Impact: API crashing 4 times daily from Out-Of-Memory (OOM), dropped WebSocket sessions, mobile app disconnects.
  • → PM-04 · Departed Developer Handover Block: Uncommitted .env, Lost AWS Root, and Frozen CI/CD SEV-2 MAJOR · DevOps & Security Handover

    The sole lead engineer departed abruptly. Production environment secrets were missing from Git, the primary AWS root was bound to the developer’s personal email, and the CI/CD pipeline depended on private uncommitted runner scripts.

    Duration: 3-week freeze → 48h full recovery · Impact: Zero deployments possible for 3 weeks; production hotfixes stalled; critical security exposure from active ex-developer keys.
