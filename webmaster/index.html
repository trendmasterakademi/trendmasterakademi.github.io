Frequently Asked Questions

What is the core difference between a custom-coded website and a WordPress/template site?

Ready-made templates load tens of thousands of lines of unused code and third-party plugins to cater to every possible user, resulting in sluggish performance, frequent security patching, and a generic look. A custom-coded website contains only clean code purpose-built for your business—delivering superior speed, security, and bespoke visual authority.

Who owns the source code and intellectual property after launch?

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of all source code, design tokens, and configuration files. We never lock clients into proprietary black-box licensing.

Our agency already designed the UI in Figma; can you handle the custom coding and Web Master engineering?

Yes. Whether we architect both the modern visual system and codebase from scratch, or take your agency's Figma designs and execute them 100% White-Label under strict NDA, we adapt seamlessly to your workflow.

How do you guarantee Google SEO performance and fast loading speeds?

Every page is built with static pre-rendering (SSR/SSG) so search engines immediately read complete semantic HTML, verified H1 hierarchies, canonical/hreflang tags, and JSON-LD structured data without waiting for heavy client-side bundles.

Do you provide ongoing server, SSL, and Web Master maintenance after launch?

Yes. Our Web Master pillar is designed not just to launch and leave, but to serve as your long-term senior technical desk for DNS, SSL/TLS, Cloudflare hardening, backups, and new feature modules.
